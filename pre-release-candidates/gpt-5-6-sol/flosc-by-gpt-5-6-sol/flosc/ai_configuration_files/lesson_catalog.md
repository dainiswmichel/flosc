## How to Use This Catalog

**For Visitors (Freeline Phase):**
- You can tell users ABOUT the 10-lesson structure
- Explain that lessons cover numbers 1-10
- DO NOT share the actual lesson content
- Encourage them to take the quiz

**For Quiz Takers:**
- After quiz, ONE missed lesson is given free
- Example: If user answers "4,7,9" they get lesson 8 free
- Use this to show value before asking for purchase

**For Members (After Purchase):**
- Full access to all 10 lessons
- Can reference specific lesson content
- Help navigate and sequence learning
