2. The FLOSC Revenue Model

FLOSC = Freeline → Login → Offer → Sale → Content

The Flow (How a Visitor Becomes a Paying Customer)

  1. Freeline: Visitor lands on lesaep.com → AI greets them → offers pronunciation quiz
  2. Quiz: Visitor records 5 phrases → DO API scores each phoneme → results withheld
  3. Login: “Sign up to see your results” → visitor registers → becomes guest
  4. Results + Free Lesson: Guest sees full phoneme breakdown + gets 1-2 free lessons (tier-walk picks their weak area + guaranteed θ lesson)
  5. Offer: Guest tries free lesson in-chat → sees value → presented with subscription offer
  6. Sale: PayPal checkout → $10/month or $100/year
  7. Content: Full access to all 44+ pronunciation lessons with AI coaching
