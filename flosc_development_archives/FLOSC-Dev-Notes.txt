FLOSC - Freeline, Login, Offer, Sale, Content WordPress Plugin

  FLOSC is a conversational sales funnel framework for WordPress that connects AI-powered chat interfaces with quiz-based learning and multi-provider payment systems. Built for teachers, coaches, and educators who want to convert visitors into paying customers through personalized, interactive experiences.

  The 5 Phases:

  1. Freeline - Visitors take a free quiz (no login required) to assess their skills or needs
  2. Login - Post-quiz users login to see results, receive a free lesson, and get personalized recommendations
  3. Offer - Dynamic sales pitch with countdown timers and contextual messaging based on user behavior
  4. Sale - Post-purchase onboarding to premium content with access management
  5. Content - Ongoing support and encouragement for active subscribers

  AI-Powered Conversational Interface:

  FLOSC connects multiple AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI) with a sophisticated IVR (Interactive Voice Response) system configured via markdown. The system evaluates complex conditions (user phase, quiz score, purchase history, login status) to deliver the right message at the right time. Messages include variable replacement ({name}, {score}, {product_name}), suggested replies with actions, and auto-triggered content based on events like quiz completion or purchase.

  5 Quiz Types Included:

  - Simple Scoring (comma-separated answer matching)
  - True/False (knowledge checks)
  - Multiple Choice (traditional testing)
  - Word Matching (vocabulary, classification)
  - Pronunciation (audio-based with Speech-to-Text integration)

  Multi-Provider Payment System:

  - Stripe - Credit cards, Apple Pay, subscriptions
  - Tokens - Internal credit economy for pay-per-use or gamification
  - Affiliate - Permission-based purchase intent (users get free access via affiliate commissions)

  Complete REST API:

  Process quiz submissions, check user access, manage offers, track tokens, handle webhooks, and more. Extensible architecture allows custom quiz types and payment providers through abstract base classes.

  Technology Stack:

  WordPress 5.8+, PHP 7.4+, vanilla JavaScript, REST API. Integrates with AssemblyAI, Whisper, and Deepgram for speech-to-text. Modern, responsive UI inspired by Claude.ai's conversational interface.

  FLOSC transforms static WordPress sites into dynamic, AI-driven learning and sales platforms with minimal configuration. Works out of the box with sensible defaults, yet fully customizable for advanced use cases.
